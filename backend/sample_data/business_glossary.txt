DataSense Business Glossary & Domain Context Notes

1. Financial Terms:
- Sales Amount (Revenue): The total monetary value generated from transactions, expressed in USD.
- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT): A score rated on a scale of 1.0 to 5.0 measuring customer delight. Scores above 4.5 are considered High Tier.
- Net Churn Rate: The percentage of recurring revenue lost from existing customers due to cancellations or downgrades.

2. Region Definitions:
- North Region: Headquarters territory covering Northern states. Priority region for Electronics growth.
- South Region: Primary market for Clothing and retail expansion.
- West Region: Technology and high-value Furniture customer base.
- East Region: Emerging market with high volume of units sold.

3. Product Categories:
- Electronics: High-margin hardware, gadgets, and computing devices.
- Furniture: Home and office decor, desks, and ergonomics.
- Clothing: Apparel, accessories, and seasonal merchandise.
