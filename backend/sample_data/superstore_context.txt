Sample Superstore - Business Policy & Glossary Notes

1. Regional Sales Territories & Growth Targets:
- East Region: High-density commercial market. Target segment is Corporate accounts. Discount threshold is capped at 15%.
- West Region: Technology and home office hub. Prioritize Furniture and Technology categories.
- South Region: Primary market for Consumer segment retail expansion.
- Central Region: Agricultural and manufacturing equipment distribution.

2. Product Category Definitions & Profit Margin Targets:
- Technology: High-margin hardware, phones, and copiers. Minimum target profit margin is 25%.
- Furniture: Office furniture, chairs, tables, and bookcases. Tables carry high shipping logistics overhead and often operate at slim or negative margins when discounted above 20%.
- Office Supplies: High-volume, repeatable items (Binders, Paper, Fasteners, Envelopes).

3. Discount & Order Rules:
- Standard Discount Policy: Sales representatives may apply up to 10% discount without approval.
- High Discount Penalty: Discounts exceeding 20% severely erode profit margins, especially in the Tables sub-category.
- High Priority Customers: Corporate and Home Office accounts with annual order volume > $10,000 receive priority shipping via First Class.
